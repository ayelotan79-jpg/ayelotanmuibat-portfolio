About Me

I'm Muibat Ayelotan, a social growth strategist for creators building on YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch.

I got into this work the way most strategists do: by getting obsessed with why some channels take off while others do everything "right" and still stall.

What I found is that growth is rarely a content problem, it's a strategy gap. No clear positioning. No repeatable system for turning viewers into a loyal audience. No plan behind the platform's own algorithm.

That's the gap I close. I work inside your channel's real numbers, retention curves, click-through rates, chat and comment behavior and build a growth plan around what's actually happening, not generic advice recycled across every niche. I've worked across gaming, lifestyle, education, and commerce, helping creators move from inconsistent uploads to a system that compounds.

My Approach

Why creators choose to work with me.

Mission

My mission is simple: help creators stop guessing and start growing on purpose. Too many talented creators post consistently and still plateau, not because their content is bad, but because there's no strategy behind it. I exist to close that gap, turning scattered uploads into a system that compounds views, subscribers, and revenue month over month.

Why Choose Me

Platform-native, not one-size-fits-all

YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch reward completely different behavior. I don't recycle the same playbook across all three. Each platform gets its own strategy, built around how it actually works right now.

Data-led, not guesswork

Every plan starts inside your channel's real numbers: retention curves, click-through rates, chat and comment behavior. Not generic "best practices" copied from someone else's niche.

Built for compounding growth, not a viral spike

I'm not chasing one lucky video. I build systems: scripts, hooks, thumbnail frameworks, schedules, so growth keeps building on itself long after we've finished working together.

A real process, every time

Every engagement runs through the same four stages: Audit, Strategy, Systems, Iterate. You always know exactly where you are and what's next.

Experience across niches

I've worked across gaming, lifestyle, education, and commerce, which means I've seen what actually moves the needle across different audiences, not just one corner of the internet.
